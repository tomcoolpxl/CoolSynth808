The first release of CoolSynth 808, a drum machine modelled on the Roland TR-808's circuits. It runs as a standalone app, or as a VST3 or CLAP plugin in your DAW, on 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.

TR-808 is a trademark of Roland Corporation. CoolSynth 808 is not made, approved or endorsed by Roland.

## Install

Download `CoolSynth808-Setup-v0.1.0.exe` below and run it. It installs the app with a Start menu entry, and the VST3 and CLAP plugins for your music software.

Windows may warn that the publisher is unknown, because the installer isn't code-signed. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

The first time the app starts, Windows asks whether it may use the network. Allow private networks, so the beat sync can play in time with other computers.

Uninstalling keeps your own patterns, projects and settings.

## What's in it

- The drums
  - Each of the 808's drum circuits is modelled part by part and solved as it plays, so every knob turns the part it turns on the machine.
  - Bass drum, snare, three toms or congas, rim shot or claves, hand clap or maracas, cowbell, cymbal, and open and closed hi-hats.
  - Each circuit was checked against circuit simulations of the service notes, then against recordings of two real 808s.
  - Options has the circuits' accuracy: Best, or Lighter and Lightest for a busy computer.
- The sequencer
  - Banks A to D of sixteen patterns, each with A and B halves and a fill, chained to loop or play once.
  - Each drum has its own lane and length, and each step a chance, a roll and the accent.
  - A library of starter beats for 22 genres and classic 808 beats, beside your own patterns.
- Under the lid
  - Lid lifts the lid: the panel's circuit board lights as each drum rings, and the 808's CPU board is drawn below it.
  - A click opens any drum's schematic, with its voltages and currents from its last hit.
- With other gear
  - A MIDI keyboard or pads play the drums, on notes you choose.
  - The beat sync plays it in time with CoolSynth 303 and with apps that speak the same protocol.
  - It follows an incoming MIDI clock and sends its own.
- In a DAW
  - The plugin follows the DAW's transport and tempo, and every knob and switch can be automated.
  - Each drum can have its own output.
  - Drag MIDI drags the pattern into the DAW as a MIDI file.
- Export audio writes the pattern or the chain as a WAV: cut, ringing on or as a seamless loop, with a file per drum if you like.

## Files

- `CoolSynth808-Setup-v0.1.0.exe` is the installer. Start here.
- `CoolSynth808-windows-x64-v0.1.0.zip` holds the app, the VST3 and the notices, without installing.
- `CoolSynth808-windows-x64-clap-v0.1.0.zip` holds the CLAP plugin, without installing.
- `CoolSynth808-windows-x64-sha256-v0.1.0.txt` has checksums for the other files.

How to play it is in the [README](https://github.com/tomcoolpxl/CoolSynth808#readme) and the [manual](https://github.com/tomcoolpxl/CoolSynth808/blob/main/docs/manual.md).
