The second release of CoolSynth 808, a drum machine modelled on the Roland TR-808's circuits, and the first for the Mac. It runs as a standalone app, or as a VST3 or CLAP plugin in your DAW, on Windows and on Macs with Apple silicon.

TR-808 is a trademark of Roland Corporation. CoolSynth 808 is not made, approved or endorsed by Roland.

## Install

On Windows: download `CoolSynth808-Setup-v0.2.0.exe` below and run it. It updates 0.1.0 in place; your own patterns, projects and settings stay.

Windows may warn that the publisher is unknown, because the installer isn't code-signed. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

On a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 13 or newer: open `CoolSynth808-Setup-v0.2.0.pkg`. It isn't signed, so macOS blocks it once: go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. It installs for your user only, without an administrator password.

The first time the app starts, it asks whether it may use the network: allow it, so the beat sync can play in time with other computers.

## What's new

- CoolSynth 808 runs on Macs with Apple silicon: the app, the VST3 and the CLAP.

## Files

- `CoolSynth808-Setup-v0.2.0.exe` is the Windows installer. Start here on Windows.
- `CoolSynth808-Setup-v0.2.0.pkg` is the Mac installer. Start here on a Mac.
- `CoolSynth808-windows-x64-v0.2.0.zip` and `CoolSynth808-macos-arm64-v0.2.0.zip` hold the app, the VST3 and the notices; the `-clap-` zips hold the CLAP. macOS blocks the unsigned zips, so use the installer if you can.
- The `-sha256-` files have checksums for the others.

How to play it: see [the guide](https://github.com/tomcoolpxl/CoolSynth808#readme).
